# EDIBELL Food Rescue — Outcomes

- [x] **Public homepage and account access.** Recreate the reference's EDIBELL homepage with its cream background, dark-green wordmark/navigation, “Good food deserves a second chance” hero, food/leaf visual, primary donation and learn-more actions, food-donor category strip, and impact summary band. Provide the reference-style Login and Register/account-type views with email/password fields, remember/forgot affordances, login action, Google option, donor/NGO/animal-welfare choices, and navigation between access states. User request: “make the website whose frontend pages looks exactly like these.. everything same .. colour and everything. exactly the same”.

- [x] **Donor donation creation and safety journey.** Provide the Donor Dashboard with sidebar, greeting, donation/food-rescued/CO₂ metrics, quick actions, recent-donations table and upcoming pickups. Include the Create Food Donation food-details form with food name/category/quantity/unit/preparation time, temperature/food state, packaging, storage, ingredients/allergen information, food photos and Save as Draft/Next actions. Include the Pickup Details & Food Safety view with location, collection date/time, contact details, special instructions, food-safety verification checklist and step navigation. Include the Food Safety & Destination Screening summary with temperature/contamination/ingredients/allergen checks, human-food and animal-feed destination paths, suitability indications and safety warning.

- [x] **NGO marketplace and request/confirmation journey.** Provide the Available Food Donations marketplace with sidebar, search/filter/category controls, food cards, quantities, pickup times and Request/View Details actions. Include the NGO Requests & QR view with selected-donation summary, pickup QR-style tile, request status, pickup information, contacts and QR artwork download affordance. Include Donation Details & OTP/QR Confirmation with donation status, request/QR-generated/pickup/completed timeline, scan/OTP details and confirmation state.

- [x] **Admin and impact analytics.** Provide the Admin Dashboard with sidebar navigation, total donations, pending reviews, completed counts, total partners, pending organization verification, safety review queue, donation categories, recent activity and status/action controls. Provide Impact Analytics with food-rescued, meals-served and CO₂-saved KPI cards, food-rescued-over-time chart, donations-by-source and human-versus-animal-feed breakdowns, plus the reference-style closing food-waste message.

- [x] **Reference-matched visual system and responsive interactions.** Across every route match the supplied reference's warm cream canvas, forest-green controls/sidebars, pale-sage card surfaces, compact sans-serif text, thin green borders, dense table/card layout, leaf mark and status colors; keep dashboard views in the persistent green-sidebar + slim topbar composition and donation forms in numbered stepper/two-column form layouts. Use distinct generated food imagery in the hero and food listings. Ensure the listed page routes and responsive layouts exist, and make demo navigation, editable form controls, category/filter selection, request actions and donation-step progression respond locally. Ensure account-type selection, food-state selection, local food-photo previews, local draft feedback, and pickup QR-style artwork download respond locally. This is a frontend demo: authentication and records need not be persisted or connected to a backend.

- [x] **Role-specific account creation and login.** The user requested: “There are actually three sections. One is for the donor, one for the NGO partner and one is animal health organization. So when a person creates an account in one particular section, that should open completely.” Show three explicit account choices—Food Donor, NGO Partner, and Animal Welfare Organization—then a role-specific account form. When the user logs in, let them select the account type, match their saved demo profile, and open the same role's landing dashboard rather than always sending them to the donor route. Do not persist passwords or imply production authentication.

- [x] **Separate donor, NGO, and animal-welfare portals.** The user requested: “So now it's like all of it is combined, all three features are combined together so it should be separate depending on what I log in I should get the page.” Provide a dedicated donor dashboard, a dedicated NGO partner dashboard and an animal-welfare dashboard, each with its own sidebar and role-relevant functions. The animal portal includes animal food requirements, available animal feed, requests/QR, received donations and notifications; the NGO portal includes available human food, NGO requests/QR and received donations; the donor portal retains creating/managing food donations and viewing donor impact. A logged-out visitor opening a portal route is sent to login; a signed-in account opening a different role's route is returned to its own portal. Shared Impact Analytics uses the active role's sidebar. Keep route links and page content role-specific and responsive.
